For your first use, complete one text-organizing task whose result you can check directly. This example needs no social media account and no search service.

## 1. Sign in to the workspace

Open [Jingya Agent](https://saas.theaicommunity.net/) and enter the tenant code, account and password your admin gave you. If the page asks you to set a new password, finish that first. If sign-in fails, see [Accounts and sign-in](/docs/getting-started/accounts).

## 2. Install "Material organization and citation"

Open **Skills** on the left and search for "Material organization and citation". Open its details to check the method, source and version, then click **Install**. Return to the workbench and select this skill for the current round. A workbench round selects at most 3 methods.

## 3. Send a real piece of material

Make sure the workbench is in "continuous conversation". Replace the sample information below with your own material, then send:

> Using the "Material organization and citation" method, split the following material into known facts, items to confirm, and two short copy versions. Rely only on the information I provide; do not invent dates, headcounts or results. Material: we are preparing a community learning exchange; the date, venue and registration method are not yet decided. Give me a draft first; do not create a publish task.


![Material organization and citation selected before sending](/img/tutorials/20261023/15-skill-chat-before-send.png)

*2026-10-23 real local flow: confirm the method tag for this round before sending.*


## 4. Check the result

Verify that facts were not rewritten, that missing information is kept as "to confirm", and that the output is clearly a draft. To change something, point at the sentence and the goal directly; there is no need to reinstall the skill.


![Text model returns two drafts](/img/tutorials/20261023/16-skill-real-output.png)

*2026-10-23 real text model output: items to confirm are preserved; nothing was published at this step.*


## 5. Export a file when you need one

Install and select the **Office documents** plugin, then send "generate a Word file from the organized result above, keeping the items to confirm". Check the file generation confirmation card, confirm, and download the file. Full steps are in [Assets, images and files](/docs/guides/assets).

You are done when you have obtained and checked one draft or file. Connecting platform accounts and starting continuous tasks can come afterwards; see [Create your first task](/docs/guides/first-task).
