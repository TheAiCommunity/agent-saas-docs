title: Accounts and sign-in

Your Jingya account gets you into the workspace; WeChat, Feishu, GitHub and other accounts are for connecting external platforms. They sign in and authorize separately.

## Prepare your sign-in information

Get the **tenant code, sign-in account and initial password** from your team's Jingya admin. The tenant code selects the workspace; do not mistake the internal tenant ID for the tenant code. The sign-in account can be the username your team assigned or a registered email or phone number, whichever your admin enabled.

## Sign in and change your password the first time

1. Open the [product sign-in page](https://saas.theaicommunity.net/).
2. Fill in tenant code, account and password, and click "Sign in".
3. If "Set a new password" appears, enter the current and new password, then save.
4. After entering the workbench, confirm you see the entrances your team expects.

## Features missing after sign-in

Feature entrances depend on role permissions and service configuration. If an install, company configuration or admin entrance is missing, ask your team admin to check your permissions. Do not use a colleague's account, and never submit passwords, tokens or app keys in tutorials or chat.

## Expired sessions and password problems

Sign in again when a session expires. If the first password change is not done, finish what the page asks first. If you forgot the password or do not know the tenant code, your team admin checks it; do not keep guessing other workspaces' codes.

## Company configuration and personal connections

Company admins maintain developer apps and service configs under **Plugins → Company integrations**; employees afterwards connect their own accounts or confirm access scopes for their own needs. A saved company configuration does not mean every employee has authorized.
